# Sprint 04 — motion and language-aware typography

- Anton is the English display face for headings, navigation, wordmarks, quotes, prices, and prominent contact text.
- Comfortaa remains loaded locally and automatically becomes the display face inside Greek (`lang="el"`) content.
- Inter remains the body and interface typeface in both languages.
- The language-aware CSS contract allows the future Greek translation to switch typography without duplicating components.
